# PRIV-14 — Bozza richiesta DPA e informazioni privacy a Northflank

Stato: bozza da approvare prima dell'invio

Destinatario: legal@northflank.com

Oggetto: Request for GDPR Data Processing Agreement — CalcoloMediazione.it

## Testo

Dear Northflank Legal Team,

I use Northflank to host CalcoloMediazione.it and its PostgreSQL database in
the Europe - West (London) region. The service may process personal data on
behalf of legal professionals and mediators.

For my GDPR Article 28 compliance records, please provide or identify the
Data Processing Agreement applicable to my current account and confirm:

1. the legal entities acting as processor and any relevant subprocessors;
2. the locations used for application workloads, database data, logs,
   support access and backups;
3. the transfer mechanism for any processing outside the EEA or the United
   Kingdom;
4. subprocessor change notification and objection procedures;
5. incident notification commitments;
6. deletion and return procedures at termination;
7. backup retention, restore process and any stated RPO/RTO;
8. how I may obtain current SOC 2 Type 2 documentation;
9. whether a countersigned or self-service DPA is available for my plan.

Please also indicate where I can subscribe to updates to the subprocessor
list and privacy terms.

Kind regards,

Carlo Alberto Calcagno  
CalcoloMediazione.it

## Istruzioni

Non inserire nella richiesta password, chiavi API, stringhe di connessione,
backup o dati di clienti. Conservare la risposta e il DPA in un archivio
riservato, annotando la data di ricezione e la versione del documento.
